## Cookies

The website sets no cookies, so it asks for no consent.

## Preferences stored in your browser

The light or dark theme and the accent colour that you choose are stored in your browser's local storage under the keys `pref-theme`, `pref-hue` and `pref-accent`. They are never sent to a server, and you can delete them by clearing the site data in your browser.

## Third-party services

Loading a page sends your IP address to the services that deliver parts of it:

- **GitHub Pages**, which hosts the website, and may log IP addresses for security ([GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement));
- **Google Fonts**, which delivers the fonts ([Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy));
- **unpkg**, which delivers the icon library ([unpkg](https://unpkg.com/)).

## People on this website

The People page shows the names, roles, projects and photographs of current and former group members, published with their consent. To have your entry changed or removed, write to the address above.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. The [Imperial College London privacy notice](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/about-the-site/privacy/) explains these rights and how to contact the Data Protection Officer.

I helped organize the [joint 2nd ERCOFTAC SIG54 workshop on "Machine Learning for Fluid Dynamics" and the EuroMech Colloquium on "Data-Driven Fluid Dynamics"](https://629.euromech.org/629/) in London. The two-and-a-half day event brought together 200 participants from across the fluid dynamics and machine learning communities, with talks and discussions spanning turbulence modelling, reduced-order models, and data assimilation. As local organizer, I was involved in the day-to-day running of the event, from logistics to chairing sessions, and enjoyed seeing the lively exchange of ideas between the two communities.

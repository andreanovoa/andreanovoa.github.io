## PhD students

Please keep an eye on the **[PhD openings at Imperial's Aeronautics Department](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/aeronautics/study/phd/phd-opportunities-and-funding/)**, or get in touch directly.

Competitive scholarships worth knowing about:

- [President's PhD Scholarship](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/postgraduate-doctoral/grants-scholarships/presidents-phd/#how-to-apply) — fully funded, applications usually close early in the year.
- Departmental and EPSRC studentships, advertised on the page above.

## Visiting and undergraduate students

- **[Erasmus+ Traineeship](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/apply/visiting-students/)** — for students registered at a European university.
- **[Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP)](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/urop/)** — summer research placements for Imperial undergraduates.

For either, please get in touch.

## MSc and MEng projects

Project topics are offered each year through the Aeronautics Department. If you are an Imperial student interested in data assimilation, reduced-order modelling or scientific machine learning, contact me before the project fair.

## Getting in touch

Write to **[a.novoa@imperial.ac.uk](mailto:a.novoa@imperial.ac.uk)**. A short note about what you have worked on and what you would like to work on is much more useful than a generic CV drop. Please mention the specific topic or paper that caught your interest.

Decimal to binary

Whole part: subtract the largest power of 2 that fits; each power used gives a bit 1, each power skipped a bit 0, so the bits are found from the largest down to 20. Fractional part: the same, with 2−1 = 0.5, 2−2 = 0.25, … Each step finds one bit, left to right.

Result: each bit counts a power of 2

Subtract powers of 2, largest first: 2M, …, 20, 2−1, 2−2, …

Keys: → step, ← back, p play, r reset, End show all

Newton's divided differences

Each step computes one entry of the divided-difference table from the two entries beside it. The top diagonal holds the coefficients: each new one adds a term ck(x − x0)…(x − xk−1) that is zero at every earlier point, so the earlier fit is kept and only corrected at the new point.

Click the graph to add a point · drag to pan · scroll to zoom

Points are used in the order listed (2 to 8 points, distinct x). Adding a point keeps every entry already computed: only its new diagonal is needed.

Newton's divided differences

Each step computes one entry of the divided-difference table. The top row gives the coefficients: every new one adds a term ck(x − x0)…(x − xk) that is zero at all the earlier points.

Drag to pan · scroll to zoom

Points are used in the order listed. Between 2 and 8 points with distinct x.

Keys: → step, ← back, p play, r reset
